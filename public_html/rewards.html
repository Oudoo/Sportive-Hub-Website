How it works

Three steps. No stamps, no cards, nothing to remember: your points follow your sessions.

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Sign up in a minute, or ask the front desk for your sign-in link if you are already a patient. You get your own referral code straight away.

  2. 2

    Earn points every session

    Physiotherapy, EMS, IV therapy, cupping, lymphatic drainage, women's health: every attended session adds 10 points. Silver members earn 25% more, Gold members 50% more.

    Redeem in your portal

    Choose a reward on your phone, show the voucher code at the front desk, enjoy. Points never expire.

Good to know

  • Do points expire?

    No. They stay until you spend them.

  • I'm already a patient. How do I get in?

    Ask the front desk, or message us on WhatsApp: we send you a sign-in link where you set your own password.

  • How do I use a reward?

    Redeem it in your portal; you get a voucher code like RW-7KQ2MXP4. Show it when you arrive and the team applies it.

  • Is my data safe?

    Your account is protected by HTTPS, optional two-step sign-in, and we only store what the programme needs: your name, contact details and your sessions.
