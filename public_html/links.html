Why clients stay

Why people stay instead of switching.

Most clinics treat one thing. You end up with a physio here, a massage there and a drip somewhere else, and nobody sees the whole picture. That's the reason our clients don't leave.

  1. 01

    One roof, one plan

    Physio, lymphatic drainage massage, EMS, IV therapy, cupping, women's health and nutrition work from the same assessment. You never start again from zero at a new clinic.

  2. 02

    A specialist for your body

    Five physiotherapists, each with a specialism: sports injury, orthopaedic rehab, women's health and post-partum, neuro, rheumatology. You see the one who knows your problem best.

  3. 03

    Built for a course, not a one-off

    Real change takes more than one visit, so our packages reward sticking with it: 6 lymphatic sessions for the price of 5, 12 for the price of 9, and physio courses of 6 or 12.

  4. 04

    Your plan keeps going at home

    Between visits you get the exercises for the area you're working on. Try the body map and see them for yourself.

  5. 05

    Booking takes one message

    No forms, no apps. Send us a WhatsApp, pick a time, and you're in.
