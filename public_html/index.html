How recovery actually gets built

Whether you're training for a race, getting back from surgery, or carrying a newborn and a sore back, the order is the same. Assess first, treat second, train third, then keep it.

It's the same sequence our physiotherapists use with professional athletes, and it's what every patient gets from day one.

Start with an assessment
Step 01

Assess, no guesswork

A licensed physiotherapist takes your history, watches you move, and finds the actual cause. You leave the first visit knowing what's wrong and what the plan is.

Every patient, every time
Step 02

Treat the cause

Hands-on physiotherapy, cupping or lymphatic drainage, matched to the assessment. Pain comes down before anything gets loaded.

Physio · Cupping · Drainage
Step 03

Rebuild strength

EMS sessions and guided exercise bring the muscle back without stressing the joint. Twenty minutes, coached, measured against where you started.

EMS · Guided exercise
Step 04

Keep it

A maintenance rhythm you can actually hold: a monthly drainage or drip, a weekly EMS slot, a physio check when something changes. Recovery as a routine, not an emergency.
